Introduction to Spatial Analysis and Geoprocessing

What is Spatial Analysis?

Spatial analysis is the process of studying geographic data to understand patterns, relationships, and trends based on location. It helps answer questions such as: Where are things happening?, What overlaps?, and What areas are most suitable?

What is Geoprocessing?

Geoprocessing is the set of GIS operations used to create new layers from existing ones. These operations transform, combine, summarize, and extract information from spatial data.

One-layer techniques
  • Buffer (point, line, polygon, multisize)
  • Dissolve & Dissolve Buffer
  • Convex Hull
  • Random Points
Multi-layer techniques
  • Intersect
  • Union
  • Difference
  • Symetric Difference
