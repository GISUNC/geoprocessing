Introduction to Spatial Analysis and Geoprocessing

Spatial Analysis

Geographic data allows us to understand patterns and relationships based on location. With spatial analysis, we can interpret geographic data in diverse ways.

Geoprocessing

Geoprocessing creates new GIS layers from existing ones. There are many basic geoprocessing operations that can be combined to produce a specific dataset for spatial analysis. We will explore them in the following examples.

One-layer techniques
  • Buffer (point, line, polygon, multisize)
  • Dissolve & Dissolve Buffer
  • Convex Hull
  • Random Points
Multi-layer techniques
  • Intersect
  • Union
  • Difference
  • Symetric Difference
